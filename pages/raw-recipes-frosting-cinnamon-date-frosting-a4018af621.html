Cinnamon Date Frosting

~ raw, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free ~

Cinnamon Date Frosting is whole food, naturally sweetened, thick and sticky frosting. It’s perfect for decorating cakes, cookies, or even dipping sliced apples into.

Medjool Dates

Medjool dates are a deep amber-brown with a crinkly jacket that shimmers from natural sugar crystals. As your teeth sink into the soft, sticky flesh, you will notice hints of a deep rich caramel, hints of wild honey and a touch of cinnamon.

Melt-in-your-mouth Medjools are so luscious they taste as if they have been warmed in an oven.” Mother Natures miracle candy!

Ingredients:   

Yields 2 cups

  • 2 cups Medjool dates, pitted
  • 1/2 cup water (maybe)
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground Ceylon cinnamon
  • 1/8 tsp Himalayan pink salt

Preparation:

  1. Remove the pits from the Medjool dates.
    • If the dates are hard, soak them in 1/2 cup of hot water for 10-20 minutes.
    • Once softened place the dates and soak water in the food processor, fitted with the “S” blade.
    • It is best not to add any water if you don’t have to. It will all depend on how soft and moist the Medjool dates are that you have.
  2. Add the cinnamon and salt.  Process until the batter turns a creamy caramel color.  This can take about 2-3 minutes.
  3. If you have a Vitamix with a tamper… perfect, this tool will make your life easier. If not, you will need to keep stopping the machine to scrape the batter into towards the blades.
  4. Frost your special treat.  Store any left-overs in the fridge for several weeks or in the freezer for months.
