Salted “Caramel” Ganache Truffles

~ raw, vegan, gluten-free ~

Ingredients:

Yields roughly 50 truffles
  • 3/4 cup raw dark agave syrup or maple syrup
  • 3/4 cup raw cacao powder
  • 1/3 cup raw cold-pressed coconut oil, melted
  • 1/8 tsp Himalayan pink salt
  • 50 large Medjool dates, pitted
  • 1/2 cup almonds, crushed
  • Coarse salt for topping

Preparation:

  1. With a sharp knife, make a slit on one side of the date and remove the pit. Set aside.
  2. In a high-powered blender, combine the sweetener, cacao powder, coconut oil, and salt.  Process until smooth.
  3. Scoop the ganache into a piping bag fitted with a round piping tip.
  4. Holding a date in your hand, pinch it open slightly.  Place the piping tip inside of the date and gently squeeze.  Be careful that you don’t overfill the date.  You want to be able to close the date without ganache seeping out.
  5. With your fingers, paint the outside of the date with a thin layer of ganache then roll into almonds.
  6. Sprinkle the top with coarse sea salt and place the truffle in a small cupcake liner and package.
  7. These will keep on the countertop in a sealed container for 5-7 days, maybe even longer.  You can freeze them for 1-3 months.  They won’t freeze solid, so this is my preferred way of preserving them.
