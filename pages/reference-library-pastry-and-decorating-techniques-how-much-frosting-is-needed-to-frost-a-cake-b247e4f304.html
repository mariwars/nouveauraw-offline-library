How Much Frosting is Needed to Frost a Cake

The suggested amounts below are estimates only.  The amount required will depend on how thick you spread the frosting and how much decorating is done.

The measurements are for covering the top, sides, and in between the layers (if layered).  Most standard recipes will make between 3 and 4 cups of frosting.  If the frosting is too stiff to spread, try placing the bowl into another bowl of warm water.  Stir to soften the frosting to desired spreading consistency.

If you are trying to determine how much frosting you need and are questioning whether you will need additional or not, you should probably go ahead and make more. The last thing you want to happen is to get almost done decorating your cake, cupcakes, or cookies and then run out of frosting.  It would be better to end up with leftovers than to run out.  :)

Round Layered Cakes:

  • 6 inch – 3 cups
  • 8 inch – 3 1/2 cups
  • 9 inch – 4 cups
  • 10 inch – 5 cups
  • 12 inch – 7 cups
  • 14 inch – 9 cups

Square Layered Cakes:

  • 6 inch – 3 1/2 cups
  • 8 inch – 4 cups
  • 10 inch – 6 cups
  • 12 inch – 7 1/2 cups
  • 14 inch – 9 1/2 cups

Other Shaped Cakes:

  • 9 x 13 x 2 – 4  cups

Cupcakes:

  • 12 cupcakes – 1 cup

Top only:

  • 8 inch or 9 inch –  ½ to 1 cup
  • 13 X 9 sheet cake –  1 ½ to 2 cups
