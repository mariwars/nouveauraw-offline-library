Today, I created a zesty blend of my own Mexican seasonings.  It took my Raw Lentil Tacos from ordinary to a fiesta of flavor.  My spice mix has a hearty flavor that’s not too spicy but is warm enough to complement my cravings for South of the Border dishes.

Ingredients:

Yields 3 Tbsp

  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp smoked  paprika
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • pinch ground cinnamon

Preparation:

  1. Mix all ingredients in a mason jar, tighten lid and shake together.
  2. This recipe will make extra for future creations.  I have a feeling that you will want to use this again. :)
