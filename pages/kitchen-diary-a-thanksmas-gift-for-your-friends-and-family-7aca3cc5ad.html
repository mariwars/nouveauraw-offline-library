A Thanksmas Gift for Your Friends and Family

Today, I want to take a moment to express my gratitude to all of you who have been so supportive of my passion here at Nouveauraw. This site has been in existence since 2007, and it has flourished into my favorite playground.

Back in 2016, we become a paid membership-based site to help support my work.  Many of you have been with me for years and some for as little as a week. Regardless, your “presence” here humbles and honors me. So thank you!

As I shared last week, we are in the midst of celebrating our family Thanksmas. You can read about (here), but in short, it’s the combination of Thanksgiving and Christmas. It’s a holiday that we celebrate when we can’t pull our families together for the two set holidays. And it just so happens that now is the right time to make this glorious event happen.

As a special thank you for your support and encouragement, we would like to extend a gifting opportunity to all our paid members only.

With the holiday season quickly approaching, I have created some gift certificates that can be printed out or emailed to friends or family as a gift. The certificate will give each person that you gift it to a FREE TWO MONTH MEMBERSHIP!

So How does this Gift Certificate Work?

  • Just print out the certificates you like. Select one, two, three, or all of them. You can gift to as many people as you wish.
  • Once printed, simply fold in half like a greeting card. These look AMAZING printed on high-gloss paper as well as white cardstock paper.
  • You can also send them as a virtually through email or text.
  • Give them to family members, friends, acquaintances, neighbors, your favorite delivery person… to anyone that you want to inspire and encourage in a healthier way of eating that is fun, delicious, and nutritious.
  • The subscription must be activated by January 31st, 2019.
