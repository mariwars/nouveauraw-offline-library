Ingredients:

  • Vegan Chunky Eggless “Egg” Salad
  • Za’atar Middle Eastern Crispy Tart Crusts

Veggies:

  • Fresh greens
  • Sprouts
  • Cherry tomatoes

Preparation:

  1. Lay a bed of greens inside of the tart.  This will create a barrier between the “egg” salad and crust.  This way things won’t get soggy.
  2. Then spread a healthy dose of “egg” salad into the crust.
  3. Top with sprouts, maybe some sliced cherry tomatoes and Enjoy!
