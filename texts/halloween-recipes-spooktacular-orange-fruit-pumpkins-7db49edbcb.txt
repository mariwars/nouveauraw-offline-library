	Spooktacular Orange Fruit Pumpkins

													    
    


				~ raw, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free ~

Have a healthier Halloween celebration with these adorable Spooktacular Orange Pumpkins.  Simply peel the orange, in this case, I used little mandarin oranges, poke a cinnamon stick piece into the top, and garnish with a mint leaf.  That’s it!  They add a fresh flavor and color to any table setting.   I hope you enjoy this simple and easy idea. Blessings, amie sue
