Ingredients:


Vegan Chunky Eggless “Egg” Salad

Za’atar Middle Eastern Crispy Tart Crusts


Veggies:


Fresh greens

Sprouts

Cherry tomatoes


Preparation:


Lay a bed of greens inside of the tart.  This will create a barrier between the “egg” salad and crust.  This way things won’t get soggy.

Then spread a healthy dose of “egg” salad into the crust.

Top with sprouts, maybe some sliced cherry tomatoes and Enjoy!
