	Almond Butter Morning Porridge

													    
    


				

~ raw, vegan, gluten-free ~

I just adore almond butter!  This porridge is so scrumptious and will give you the fuel to get your day started!  I blended this recipe into a porridge consistency, but if you would rather have it be a bit chunkier, just mix it by hand.  You could also use any kind of nut butter that you prefer.  You just can’t go wrong with nut butters.

Ingredients:

Yields 1 serving


1/3 cup rolled, gluten-free oats

1 cup almond milk

2  Tbsp raw almond butter (1 tbsp blended, 1 for serving)

1 ripe banana (1/2 for blending, 1/2 for garnish)

2 Tbsp chia seeds

Stevia to sweeten (agave, honey, yacon syrup)


Garnish ideas:


raisins

1 Tbsp nut butter

banana slices

strawberries

blueberries


Preparation:


Combine all ingredients into the blender, except for 1/2 a sliced banana and raisins and mix well.

Allow mixture to rest overnight in the fridge or allow it to sit on the counter for 1 hour.

Garnish and enjoy!
