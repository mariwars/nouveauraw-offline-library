	Reuben Crostini

													    
    


				

All of the components needed to create this recipe mashup are wonderful on their own, but they can also be used in many other ways besides making crostinis with them.  Just feeding your noggins with ideas, that’s all. :)

Eating a high raw diet, or even just adding some raw foods into your diet doesn’t have to leave you bored and craving comfort foods.

Raw comfort foods can be just as satisfying as cooked foods, and do I dare say… even more so?!   Not only can they satisfy the taste buds, but they also nourish the body in ways most processed foods lack.  I hope you enjoy this recipe, mashup!   Amie Sue

Ingredients:


Vegan Swiss Cheese

Raw Caraway and Dill Sauerkraut

Raw Thousand Island Cole Slaw

Caraway and Dill Crispy Flatbread


Preparation:


Layer each component on the flatbread.  Pile it as high as you want. Enjoy!
