	Apple Butter Spread

													    
    


				
~ raw, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free ~




Apple Butter Spread is a great staple to have in the fridge.  It is wonderful on breads, crackers, in smoothies and even in porridge recipes.  Not to mention just eating it just by the spoonful when the munchies set in.

  


The ground chia helps this spread to set up.   I used Pink Lady apples which really lends a scrumptious sweet taste.  So when you make this recipe just keep in mind that the type of apple that you use will affect the flavor.  You also may need to adjust the sweeteners that you use based on the level of sweetness of the apples.   Enjoy, amie sue

Ingredients:



3 apples, cored, peeled, rough chopped

2 Tbsp raw agave nectar

2 Tbsp date paste or 2 Medjool dates

5 tsp chia seed, ground

1 tsp fresh lemon juice

1 1/2 tsp ground Ceylon cinnamon

½ tsp ground nutmeg

½ tsp ground clove

pinch Himalayan pink salt



Preparation:



In a blender combine the apples, agave, dates, lemon juice, ground chia, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and salt. Process until smooth.  The butter will thicken as it sits due to the chia.

Store in a glass jar in the fridge for up to 5-7 days.
