Caramelized Onion Corn Bread

Add to favorites

~ raw, vegan, gluten-free ~

Bread, bread, bread, bread, bread, bread, bread!
I seriously couldn’t be happier to have bread back in our lives. When I created this recipe, I had one mission: I wanted the corn flavor to stand up to those caramelized onions. I wanted texture, aroma that makes your mouth water, and above all, I wanted it to taste amazing. Check, check, check… and check!

Dried corn — the unsung hero

I grabbed some dried corn from my pantry, patiently waiting to be used, but I also craved that sweet, plump pop of fresh corn kernels every time I bit into the bread. The combo of dried and fresh corn gave the perfect balance of texture and flavor — it’s like the best of both worlds in every bite.

Spongy bread magic

If you’re new to psyllium, let me introduce you to your new best friend. This tiny seed is a powerhouse, known for its high fiber content and amazing binding ability. It’s the secret behind this bread’s lovely spongy texture — basically, it’s gluten’s stand-in here.

You’ll find psyllium in raw breads, smoothies, sauces, pie crusts—you name it. But heads up: don’t go overboard or your recipe could get a little weird in texture.

Bonus: it’s a natural source of soluble fiber and can help with digestive ups and downs — constipation, diarrhea, you name it. Just remember to drink plenty of water when you’re eating dried foods. It’s a smart habit anyway!

This bread is absolutely fantastic on its own—no spreads needed. But if you want to get fancy, try mixing equal parts softened coconut butter and raw honey. Pure heaven, trust me. blessings, amie sue

Ingredients:

Yields 1 loaf

Dry Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rolled, gluten-free oats, ground to flour
  • 1 cup dried corn, ground
  • 1/4 cup psyllium husks
  • 1/4 cup + 2 Tbsp flax, ground to flour
  • 3 Tbsp coconut flour
  • 1 tsp Himalayan pink salt

Wet Ingredients:

  • 2 cups packed, moist almond pulp
  • 1 cup Caramelized Onions, chopped
  • 2 cups organic corn kernels
  • 1 cup raw almond milk
  • 2 Tbsp raw honey or coconut nectar for vegan

Preparation:

  1. Making oat flour:  first, make your oat flour but putting the oats in the food processor, processing until it reaches an excellent flour consistency.
  2. Add ground dried corn, psyllium, ground flax, coconut flour, and salt.  Pulse till mixed — place in a large bowl.
  3. Add almond pulp, caramelized onions, corn kernels, almond milk, and honey.
    • Mix with your hands, making sure everything is well combined.
    • Depending on how moist your almond pulp is, you may need to add water, so the dough sticks together nicely.  If you do this, add 1 Tbsp at a time.
  4. Remove the batter and shape it into the desired size.  I pressed mine into a loaf pan and popped it out to get the shape.
  5. Dehydrate at 145 degrees for 1 hour to set the outer crust,
  6. Remove and slice the bread into 1″ thickness.  Lay them flat on the mesh tray.
  7. Reduce the temp to 115 degrees and continue to dry for 6-10 hours.
    • It is up to you how much moisture you want to be left in the slices. So keep an eye on them and document that time frame for future loaves of raw bread.
  8. Side note – Although not raw, this bread toasts up nicely.  Just ask my husband.  haha
