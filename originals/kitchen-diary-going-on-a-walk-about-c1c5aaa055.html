Going on a “Walkabout”

Add to favorites

Good day everyone. Here we are just a few days into the new year of 2016!  I am so excited for what this year has to offer.

I will keep this brief but I wanted to let you know that for the first time since Nouveau Raw began, that I won’t be available for the next month or so… oy-vey, I just might experience withdrawals. lol

It has been my daily routine every day for well over six years to snuggle up with my computer pouring over your comments, questions and emails. Making sure to respond to all of them. I adore each and every one of you and have made so many amazing friends through my site.  However….

This Friday, on the 8th of January, I am detaching from all that I am today, in hopes of returning even stronger, more enlightened, and grounded…   Thus the term walkabout comes into play.

“A walkabout requires a decision to take a first step, and a conscious and intentional movement  (inwardly, outwardly or both) in the general direction of a longing or calling — with an openness to the actual experience, and  a minimal attachment to expectations and results.” Betsy Lewis

Betsy, said it perfectly.  Please feel free to leave comments and ask your questions on recipe posts and once I am back, I will answer them all.  I have a recipe post scheduled to be released every Wednesday while I am gone so that should keep you busy while I am away. hehe That and the 1,200+ recipes already available to you.  Many blessings, be well.  amie sue

      I think the first thing that I would do, is to get yourself organized.. which it sounds like you are already in the midst of that. As far as building up your pantry / fridge / freezer… I would start with getting all the nuts and seeds soaked and dehydrated. This will be helpful for the ease of quick snacking and for when recipe inspiration attacks. :) Click here if you need some guidance on that: https://nouveauraw.com/soaking-nuts-seeds-and-grains/.

      If you have the fridge or freezer space… I would store the dehydrated nuts/seeds in jars in the fridge or freezer to protect them from going rancid. If you think you will use them within a month, the pantry should be just fine.

      I have made it a habit over the years to soaked and dry all nuts/seeds the moment I bring them home. Now it just second nature.

      When it comes to stocking up on raw goods; bars, cookies, granolas, cereals, crackers, and raw breads and great because they can all be frozen for 1-3 months (depending on what they are). You can create a recipe and keep half for the up and coming week and put the remaining half in the freezer. Before you know it, you will have a supply stocked up. Be sure to store everything in airtight containers so freezer odors don’t creep in. Label and date them too.
